# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Read EPUB, FB2, PDF and DjVu from a local library, an OPDS catalog or a BookOrbit account. Switch between a single page, a two-page spread and continuous scrolling.

- Browse books by cover or in a list, search the library and resume a saved reading position. The BookOrbit dashboard includes current reads, recent additions, authors and series.
- The first time you read a BookOrbit edition, BOTUI saves it on your device. Continue from **BookOrbit offline downloads** without a connection and sync your place when you reconnect.
- Navigate chapters and footnotes, search inside books, set bookmarks and export passage notes. For scanned documents, adjust page width, rotate pages and invert their colors.
- Open cover art in your system image viewer from a book card or reading menu. The source chooser and settings open in Catppuccin Mocha on a fresh installation; settings keep the same size across tabs.
- Choose a terminal palette or a built-in theme and set page width, gutter, margins and first-line indentation. The keyboard guide and settings are also accessible with a mouse.
